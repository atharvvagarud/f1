## Scientific question

Given a causal historical RaceState(T), which alternative future race
scenarios can the platform simulate as controlled counterfactual
interventions without using post-T historical information, inventing
unsupported mechanics, or overstating causal validity?

## Counterfactual definition

- FACTUAL STATE: information observed at or before T.
- INTERVENTION: a controlled modification imposed after T.
- SIMULATED CONSEQUENCE: future state generated by MC2 after the intervention.
- HISTORICAL FUTURE: what actually happened after T — FORBIDDEN as
  simulation input; post-hoc context/evaluation only.
- Causal boundary: T. Post-T drivers are MC2-simulated or forbidden.

V3.7 estimates SIMULATOR-CONDITIONAL OUTCOMES UNDER CONTROLLED
INTERVENTIONS. It does not establish true counterfactuals, real-world
treatment effects, optimal strategy, team correctness, or history truth.

## V3.6 boundary

V3.6 provides one driver, one decision, PIT_NOW / PIT_NEXT_LAP / STAY_OUT,
compound selection, no-intervention reference, paired CRN futures,
trade-offs. Restrictions split: single-driver/decision
(SCIENTIFIC+IMPLEMENTATION), N-cap (IMPLEMENTATION), no weather/SC/
retirement (SCIENTIFIC — no mechanics), no compound pace delta
(SCIENTIFIC — deferred), tiny cache-first catalogue (PRODUCT+PERFORMANCE).

## MC2 mechanics evidence (repository-pinned)

- Kernel: never simulates DNFs/weather/SC (`mc2/kernel.py`).
- Regimes: YELLOW/VSC/SAFETY_CAR/WET/RED all DEFERRED with exact reasons
  (`regime_support.py`); RNG domains for duration/weather-transition
  reserved and unconsumed.
- Reliability: `reliability_enabled=True` rejected, not faked (R6-R/S).
- Traffic/overtaking: gated off; free-air ordering retained.
- Compound: requested-compound override exists; no pace delta (loss/age/
  stint only); V3.6D shows distinguishable effects via those channels.
- Plans: exactly one single-target intervention per run
  (`_InterventionPlan`: action, target, decision_lap, compound);
  statuses PENDING/APPLIED/SUPPRESSED/SUPERSEDED/RACE_END_CANCELLED.
- Streams: per-`(master_seed, simulation_index)` identities;
  pit-domain separation preserves pace streams across stop-count changes.

## Pit-in-N-laps

Contract rule: N ≥ 1; N ≤ remaining_laps − 1; trigger reads simulated
state; earlier baseline pit supersedes; race end cancels. No fixed small
cap (would be UI-arbitrary); B freezes window mechanics, D sets an
evidence cap if execution collapses beyond N*.

## Multi-stop plans

Compositional `CounterfactualPlan` (ordered intervention list), not
PIT_IN_2/3/… enums. Decided in A; specified in B; built in C.

## Compound semantics

Retained with disclosure: loss/age/stint channels only, no pace delta.

## Undercut/overcut

Derived only (plan-vs-plan + P(ahead of rival)). Primitives rejected.

## Retirement/reliability

Rejected. No mechanism; forbidden outcomes.

## Safety Car

Rejected. Missing: deployment trigger, duration, compression, pit-loss
changes, restart, tyre effects. Active-SC states stay ineligible.

## Weather

Rejected. Missing: stochastic evolution, transition model, tyre response.
Variables at T are historical observations; none evolve in MC2; wet
compounds unrepresentable in dry-green simulation.

## Track status/red flag

Rejected. Race-control history is observation, not a generative model.

## Competitor strategy

Admitted as B-extension: "What if Verstappen pits while Norris stays
out?" propagates through joint ordering only — never traffic battles.

## Multi-driver interventions

Admitted as B-extension with fail-closed conflicts and order-invariant,
contamination-free CRN.

## Race-state interventions

Rejected. Gaps/positions/ages/pace are facts.

## Intervention horizon / state machine / conflicts

Rule above; lifecycle PLANNED/ELIGIBLE/EXECUTED/INERT/SUPERSEDED/
CANCELLED/TERMINAL/INVALID (V3.6 vocabulary reused); conflicts fail
closed or frozen-deterministic; transitions frozen in B.

## CRN generalization

Same state/stream-family/index; schedules differ only. Pit-domain
draw-count and supersession paths are documented structural divergence
under complete-pair accounting. CRN never claimed to remove model
uncertainty.

## Reference semantics

Canonical reference unchanged. Plan-vs-plan admitted as secondary with
identical discipline. No historical baseline.

## Outcomes

V3.6 set retained. Admitted: P(A ahead of rival) (joint futures +
pair retention plumbing), pit count (tracked), position gain/loss
(transform). Research: time loss (verify retention). Rejected: expected
points (deferred), DNF probability (no generation).

## Driver-vs-driver outcomes

`P(driver A finishes ahead of driver B)` over complete pairs — joint
mutually-exclusive futures make it natural; never confused with V3.5
marginals.

## Temporal leakage threat model

## Validation limits

Possible: determinism, temporal safety, invariants, CRN reduction, seed/
count stability, sanity, sensitivity, cross-plan consistency,
plausibility. Impossible from replay alone: true counterfactual accuracy.

## Evaluation design

Layers: synthetic invariants, eligible-state sample, stress, multi-plan
consistency, seed/count, CRN, case studies, unsupported coverage.
Deterministic preregistered sampling in D.

## Product questions

"What if Norris waited three laps?", "PIT L+2 vs L+5?", "probability of
finishing ahead of Verstappen?" — only admitted-scope questions.

## Computational cost

combos). Prune on validity (eligibility, execution collapse), never on
top-strategy heuristics. No broad cache build.
